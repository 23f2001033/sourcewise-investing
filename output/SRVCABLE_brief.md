# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE)

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables makes power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% year on year; EBITDA was ₹140 crore and profit after tax was ₹82 crore. [S3]
- EBITDA margin was 11.2%, down from 12.9%, a 170 basis-point decline. [S3]

## Bull case

- The order book was ₹3,900 crore, up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier. [S3]
- Exports were 21% of revenue, up from 16%, and the Bharuch plant is expected to add about 30% cable capacity from Q3 FY27. [S3]
- Management said data-centre cables were about 9% of domestic revenue and could double in two years. [S2]
- Promoter pledge fell to 4.1% of promoter holding from 11.5% a year earlier; FII and DII ownership also increased. [S8]

## Bear case

- Only about two-thirds of contracts have copper price-variation clauses, and those clauses reset with a one-to-two-quarter lag. [S2]
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96, mainly because two state utility customers are paying more slowly. [S2]
- Bharuch capex is ₹620 crore, funded mainly by internal accruals and some term debt; net debt to equity is 0.35 times. [S2]
- Profit rose only about 6% while official revenue rose 18%, so the margin pressure is not just a headline risk. [S2]
- The NSE disclosed a ₹46.3 crore GST demand including interest and penalty for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S7]

## Open questions

- The company says it has a strong legal case and expects no material impact, but it intends to appeal and this is a company assertion rather than an independently verified outcome. [S7]
- Business Daily reported ₹1,428 crore revenue and 35% growth, conflicting with the official release's ₹1,248 crore and 18%; the official figures are preferred after arithmetic checks. [S1]
- A March 2024 article reported 35% promoter pledge, but it is stale and superseded by the July 2026 exchange filing showing 4.1%. [S4]
- What are the company's valuation, cash-flow profile, and peer-relative returns? The pack does not provide them. [S1][S2][S3][S7][S8]
- Is the ₹1,012 share price reported on 9 August still relevant on 23 September? It is too old to treat as current. [S1]
- Will copper-cost pass-through protect margins after the one-to-two-quarter lag? [S2]
